# Informed Consent Form for Expert Delphi Evaluation Panel

## Research Title
Prioritizing Educational Investment via Spatial Clustering (Sudan Case Study)

## Purpose and Procedures
You are invited to participate in a double-blind expert evaluation panel. The objective of this research is to evaluate the operational validity, social fairness, and policy enforceability of three automated spatial zoning configurations designed for educational reconstruction planning. Your participation involves reviewing three standardized spatial priority maps and scoring them across pre-defined utility indicators over up to three iterative Delphi feedback loops.

## Voluntary Participation & Anonymity Safeguards
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw from the panel at any baseline execution point without penalty. Your responses will be strictly anonymized. Individual identity descriptors will be removed from tabular records and aggregated under an alphanumeric credential token to prevent personal trackback. 

## Data Storage & Retention
All transcribed evaluation sheets will be securely stored within encrypted, access-controlled repositories. Aggregated inter-rater consensus statistics (Kendall's W Coefficient) will be published openly, but your raw, identifiable grading profile will remain confidential.

### Electronic Consent Affirmation
By proceeding to the digital evaluation instrument and executing the evaluation index matrix, you explicitly certify that you are $\ge 18$ years of age, possess the required operational background, and knowingly consent to the storage and computational processing of your anonymized review scores.
